AI Powered Small Business Verification Platform

An AI powered marketplace verification platform that helps administrators verifybusinesses using Google's Gemini AI.

Small businesses often struggle to gain customer trust online. Customers also find it difficult to determine whether an online handmade store is genuine.
This platform solves both problems by allowing business owners to register their stores while using Artificial Intelligence to assist administrators in verifying authenticity before approval.
The system does not automatically approve businesses. Instead, Gemini AI analyzes the submitted information and provides an intelligent recommendation to help administrators make informed decisions.

#  Features
## Business Registration: Businesses can
- Register their store
- Add business description
- Add social media links
- List products
- Submit verification request

## AI Business Verification: Google Gemini AI analyzes:
- Business description
- Product information
- Social media links
- Contact details
- Business category

The AI generates:
- Trust Score (0–100)
- Risk Level
- Authenticity Analysis
- Approval Recommendation
- Verification Reasons

## Description Authenticity Check
The AI evaluates whether the business description is:
- Detailed
- Genuine
- Too generic
- AI-generated
- Potentially copied
- 
## Admin Dashboard: Administrators can:
- Review pending businesses
- View AI Trust Score
- Read AI-generated verification reasons
- View uploaded images
- Approve or reject businesses
- Download verification reports

The final decision always belongs to the administrator.
